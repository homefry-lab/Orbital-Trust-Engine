About Orbital Lens

Orbital Lens tracks satellite behavioral integrity — not just position. Each operator receives a health score based on their documented response to Space-Track conjunction warnings. Green indicates nominal response history. Yellow flags delayed or absent maneuver confirmations. Red indicates active conjunction risk. Orange marks derelict or hazardous objects.
