Rating Methodology (V0.1)

● A / Nominal — Operator acknowledged CDM within 48h and confirmed maneuver or stand-down decision.
● B / Watch — CDM issued, response window open or delayed acknowledgment (>48h).
● C / Urgent — Active conjunction risk, no confirmed maneuver. TLE delta indicates no response.
● — / Hazard — Derelict or non-maneuverable. No operator contact possible.

Scores derived from TLE delta comparison against Space-Track CDM event timestamps. Private — not publicly disclosed. All ratings preliminary pending methodology peer review.
